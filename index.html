01 / THE INTRODUCTION

A little about me.

Hi! I'm a 22-year-old BAMS student here at UPOU, and I also work full-time as a Math Teacher. I teach students from 3-year-olds all the way to senior high school, so my days are basically a mix of numbers, patience, and adjusting to very different energy levels.

When I'm not studying or teaching, I'm usually reading fictional romance stories—books, manhwas, mangas, anything with a good love story. I'm also into Marvel, movies, and anime. My music taste is quite seasonal (depending on my algorithm in social media), but right now, Adela has me in a chokehold.

As for BAMS, I'm still figuring out what I really want to specialize in, but I'm currently leaning toward graphic design. AI honestly scares me a little, especially because of the environmental concerns around it, but I also want to learn how to use it wisely, with it serving as a tool that can support creativity without taking away the human thought and effort behind it.

02 / COLLABORATIVE WEB DESIGN November 18, 2026

Whisker & Wonder

My role: Group leader & main coder

Whisker & Wonder is our group's website for a T-shirt brand that promotes kindness toward stray and rescued animals. This was my first time really exploring coding, so I'm pretty proud of what we accomplished. As the group leader and main coder, I designed the homepage, privacy policy page, and mobile layouts with accessibility in mind. I also guided my groupmates as we brought our ideas together. Seeing our website finally come to life made all the time spent learning and figuring things out worth it.
